Gateway Coverage

One gateway can support up to approximately 3,000 tags within an approximate 30-meter (98-foot) radius. Actual coverage may vary by store layout and wireless conditions.

Product Warranty

CREBEE Gateway Pro SMS: 2-year factory warranty. ESL tags: 1-year factory warranty. Contact TELICA USA for warranty terms or help submitting a warranty request.

Tag Battery Life

Tag battery life is approximately 5 years. Actual battery life may vary with usage and update frequency.

FCC Certification

The product is FCC-certified for the U.S. market.

U.S. Shipping

U.S. orders ship via UPS or USPS. Shipping charges are calculated with your quote.

Applicable Sales Tax

Unopened Products

Return requests for unused products in their original, unopened packaging must be made within 30 days of delivery and approved before return shipment. Approved returns are inspected before any eligible refund is issued.

Electronic Shelf Labels

Opened or installed labels with scratches, physical damage, or other signs of use are not eligible for return for a change of mind. If an item arrives damaged, is defective, or is incorrect, contact us so we can review the issue.
